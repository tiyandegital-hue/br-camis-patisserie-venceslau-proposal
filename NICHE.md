# CAMIS Patisserie — Niche Research (artisanal patisserie / boutique bakery-café)

Rule: the niche's best-designed sites decide the visual language. Five references, all verified live or cited in 2026 design roundups.

## 1. Dominique Ansel Bakery — https://www.dominiqueansel.com/
- New York patisserie (Cronut® inventor). Verified live 2026-11-09.
- **What works:** pre-order-first homepage (this month's Cronut® flavour front and centre); "The Creations" as a signature-product gallery — each invention gets a name, a story and a photo; seasonal drops create urgency; shipping + shop in one flow.
- **Steal for CAMIS:** lead with ONE hero creation of the month; give every signature product a name and a story; pre-order CTA above the fold.

## 2. Milk Bar — https://www.milkbarstore.com/
- NYC bakery, ships nationwide. Verified live 2026-11-09.
- **What works:** loud, playful, unapologetic colour and type; close-up cross-section shots that show layers/fillings/texture inside the dessert; delivery-date picker on the product page; press-quote wall ("Word on the street") for trust; gift/occasion framing everywhere ("made for everyday celebrations").
- **Steal for CAMIS:** cross-section close-ups of cookies (show the filling); occasion framing (aniversário, presente); bold colour confidence instead of timid pastels.

## 3. Tartine Bakery — https://www.tartinebakery.com/
- San Francisco / LA artisan bakery. Verified live 2026-11-09.
- **What works:** craft-as-story: "a thoughtful expression of modern craft through good food and warm experiences"; process photography (baker icing a cake, filling tart shells) instead of only finished product; "Inside Stories" video chapters; special-order cakes with a clear 7-day lead time; cookbook heritage as authority.
- **Steal for CAMIS:** process/behind-the-scenes imagery as a design element; founder-craft story as the trust anchor; explicit lead-time rule for custom cakes.

## 4. Magnolia Bakery — https://www.magnoliabakery.com/
- NYC institution, cited in Zarla's "15 Best Bakery Website Examples" (2026) and Freshy roundups.
- **What works:** fame turned into an e-commerce machine — rotating seasonal feature hero, an "Order ASAP" button riding the nav, a scannable product grid of greatest hits (banana pudding, cupcakes, cookies) one scroll down; limited drops ("Now Available: … Collection") create repeat visits.
- **Steal for CAMIS:** scannable "greatest hits" grid; seasonal/limited flavour drop as the recurring hook; order button always visible.

## 5. Bobbette & Belle — Toronto patisserie (bobbetteandbelle.com)
- Cited in Zarla's 2026 bakery roundup: "Patisserie polish, pastel and ribboned."
- **What works:** presents like a luxury confectioner — pale airy hero of ribbon-tied cookie stacks and a single-tier cake, refined serif headline ("Toronto's Favourite Cakes & Pastries, Made from Scratch"), soft blue + cream palette that feels like a gift box; nav reveals a lifestyle brand (Everyday Cakes, Holidays, Lifestyle & Décor) — packaging and styling as much the product as the macarons.
- **Steal for CAMIS:** gift-box / present framing for cookies and cakes; "made from scratch" as the quiet quality claim; patisserie as a lifestyle brand, not just a shop.

## Synthesis — the visual language for CAMIS
- **Photography first, always:** the product is the design. Big close-ups, cross-sections, process shots. No CSS-only heroes, no stock illustrations.
- **Bold, not beige:** this niche's winners are loud (Milk Bar) or lush (Bobbette & Belle) — never the ivory/espresso house template. CAMIS gets a confectionery-atelier palette: deep cocoa, raspberry, buttercream, pistachio (see STITCH-PROMPT.md).
- **One hero creation + greatest-hits grid + occasion framing** is the proven conversion pattern across all five.
- **Founder craft story** is the trust anchor (Tartine, Dominique Ansel) — Camila's journalism + gastronomy background is the differentiator.
