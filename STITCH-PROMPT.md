## 1. PSYCHOLOGY FIRST (read before designing)
- **Visitor:** a woman in Presidente Venceslau (or nearby) planning a birthday, a gift, or a weekend café visit. She decides with her eyes in 3 seconds: "does this look delicious and special enough for my occasion?"
- **Buying emotion:** affection + celebration. CAMIS sells "afeto" (affection) — every cake is for someone's special moment. The site must feel like opening a gift box, not browsing a catalogue.
- **Trust gap:** a new shop (Dec 2025) with no website. Trust comes from: the founder's real craft story, process photography (hands, oven, chocolate), and the press feature (Portal Proezas, Sep 2026). No invented reviews, no fake ratings, no fake awards.
- **Conversion path:** Crave (hero product shot) → Believe (founder + process) → Choose (menu + occasions) → Act (Instagram DM — one tap, no forms that go nowhere).
- **One hero move per screen.** Never animate the whole frame. Restraint = premium.

## 2. VISUAL LANGUAGE — "Doce Atelier" (FRESH — never the ivory/espresso house template)
- **Palette:** deep cocoa `#2B1A12` (ink/ground), raspberry `#D6336C` (primary pop), buttercream `#FFF3D6` (light ground), pistachio `#8A9A5B` (secondary accent), caramel `#C68A3C` (warm highlight). NO ivory paper + espresso default. NO beige minimalism.
- **Type:** Fraunces (display serif, soft + wonky, for headlines and the giant CAMIS wordmark) + Archivo (sans for body/UI). Portuguese diacritics must render correctly.
- **Texture:** subtle paper-grain + hand-drawn sticker badges ("feito à mão", "chocolate belga", "encomende já") with slight rotation; a scrolling marquee strip ("Cookies artesanais • Bolos autorais • Chocolate belga Callebaut • Café •").
- **Photography-led:** every hero and card is a big, warm, close-up food photograph (cookies with visible filling cross-section, glossy chocolate cake, café corner). Rich, saturated, appetising — never flat, never stock-looking. Use real-looking AI-generated food photography in the Stitch design; the coder will replace with the AI photoshoot set.
- **Motion:** gentle and edible — slow ken-burns on hero food, sticker badges pop in with 15–30% overshoot, marquee scrolls continuously, product cards lift on hover. One hero move per moment; payoff stillness after each.
- **Layout energy:** bold hero moments — giant CAMIS wordmark overlapping the hero photo, oversized prices-free product names, generous colour blocking (cocoa sections alternating with buttercream). Kinetic, joyful, gift-box energy.
